# Blade evolution — 2026-10-22

## Balance and save contracts

- Weapon `attack` replaces percentage power. Damage scales against the starting sword's attack of 165. Black Blade: 255; Giant Greatsword: 430, speed .32 (1.34-second base normal interval). Existing run power remains a multiplier and existing saved inventories retain their IDs.
- Boss HP: dragon 2900 → 3300, demon 4600 → 5500, god 6200 → 7400, shadow 8500 → 10200. Other HP unchanged; transformations still recover to 75% and grant an over-cap potion.
- Stage XP thresholds: `round(42 + 8*(level-1) + .8*(level-1)^2)`. Ordinary-room XP factors: .85 / 1 / 1.08 / 1.18. The first level costs more; deeper costs increase gradually instead of multiplying by 1.55.
- Configured skill range/radius is averaged with normal sword reach (`125 * player.reach`) once per cast. Aim, impact, projectile lifetime, persistent fields and primary visuals use that snapshot. Unsealed Dawn retains unlimited range.
- Dawn remains available from level 1; offer weight .8 → 1.05 after demon victory. Claiming the upgrade mission changes its form, never its permanent carry ownership. Choosing Dawn in a clear chest is still valid. Already-owned carry rights and pending old chests are retained.
- Growth UI waits for anticipation, release pose, manual projectiles/fields/delayed hits and defeat animations. Final boss death still discards pending growth before rank and inheritance.

## Combat presentation

- Short anticipation on additional manual attacks; the basic crescent stays responsive. Every active skill has its own motion silhouette, while heavy signatures and ultimates keep independent effects.
- Crescent is a blue blade arc. Its third-use upgrade strengthens the cut rather than launching a shockwave.
- Boss arena width 1260; height up to 1060, bounded by the world's ends. Central solid obstacles removed; movement, safe landing, navigation, camera, aim and background agree with the larger bounds.
- Late bosses use harmless, separately drawn teleport passages; second-form god can chain three jumps, demon two. Existing warnings and exposed counter windows prevent new jumps. Shadow retains its own spinning-double teleport vocabulary.
- Arrival lasts 4.6 seconds, or 6.2 for late bosses, with a camera focus and ritual. Short-cinematic preference and skip remain available. Rage speeds up movement and attack scheduling while retaining full attack warnings.
- Duelist circles before a narrow rush; hexer keeps distance and casts at a predicted location. Other mobs gain small movement differences. Shadow versions retain neutral bodies and recognisable equipment.
